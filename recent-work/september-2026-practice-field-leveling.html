Athens, AL September 2026

Project Overview

This September we got the call to work on the practice football field at Lindsey Lane. A practice field takes a beating — ruts, low spots, and uneven ground make for poor footing and can lead to rolled ankles and puddles after every rain. The goal was to give the team a smooth, level surface they could practice on safely.

We started by leveling out the field, knocking down the high spots and filling in the low ones so the whole surface sits even. From there we went over it with the Harley rake to break up clods, pull out rocks and debris, and leave a clean, smooth seedbed that's ready for grass to take hold.

When we wrapped up, the field went from rough and uneven to a level, freshly raked surface ready for the season ahead.

What We Did

  • Practice football field leveling
  • High spot cutting and low spot filling
  • Harley raking to remove rocks and debris
  • Seedbed preparation
  • Final cleanup and grading
