Athens, AL September 2026

Project Overview

Also in September, we were back at Lindsay Lane — this time to put in gravel walking paths around their football field. Fans, families, and students needed a clean, solid place to walk around the field instead of trudging through grass and mud on game nights.

We hauled in several loads of gravel and spread it out along the path routes circling the field, working it to an even depth so the surface stays firm underfoot and sheds water instead of holding it. Keeping the edges neat and the paths consistent all the way around was key to giving it a finished look.

The result is a set of clean, durable gravel walkways that make getting around the football field easier and safer for everyone.

What We Did

  • Gravel delivery — several loads
  • Gravel spreading for walking paths
  • Even depth for a firm, well-drained surface
  • Paths around the football field
  • Final cleanup
