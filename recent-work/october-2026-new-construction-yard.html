Ardmore, AL October 2026

Project Overview

This October we finished up a new construction yard in Ardmore. After the house goes up, the ground around it is usually left rough — low spots, construction debris, and compacted clay that won't drain or grow grass well. The homeowners needed a finished yard that slopes away from the house and is ready for grass.

We hauled in 20 loads of topsoil to backfill around the house and fill in the low areas. With the topsoil in place, we graded the entire yard so it drains properly and has a smooth, even finish.

To wrap it up, we seeded the yard and covered it with straw to hold in moisture, protect the seed, and keep the fresh topsoil from washing out until the grass takes hold.

What We Did

  • Hauled in 20 loads of topsoil
  • Backfilled around new construction
  • Final yard grading for proper drainage
  • Grass seeding
  • Straw covering for erosion control
